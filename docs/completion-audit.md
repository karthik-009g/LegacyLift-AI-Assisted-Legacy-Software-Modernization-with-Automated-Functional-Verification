# Initial prototype completion audit — 2026-11-16

## Scope and source

This audit follows the original [project brief](project-brief.md), including its eight milestones and deliberately small scope: one controlled Flask/SQLite reference and three stateful ticket workflows reconstructed in FastAPI. Later session requirements add cloud providers, primary/backup selection, local Docker isolation, and local-only development until a separately authorized push.

The brief was recovered from its complete earlier tool output in this chat after the Downloads file disappeared. Its recovered text has SHA-256 `e282bc3005fb7e780f0db6a1851939ed246d28439497a8b25fcf9408a927c25b`. The proposed directory tree is explicitly illustrative; equivalent implemented modules are mapped below rather than adding empty files.

The brief requires a working evidence/verification prototype and an honest controlled experiment. It specifies no minimum model accuracy and explicitly treats hybrid superiority as a hypothesis. Failed generated candidates are therefore recorded negative outcomes. They are not evidence of successful modernization.

## Limits of completion

The initial prototype is complete within the brief's controlled scope. It does not automate arbitrary enterprise migrations, establish model accuracy across repositories, provide production authentication, or prove security against hostile code. Generated candidates remain demonstrably incorrect; the project detects and reports those failures. Further model trials, a timed human correction study, production deployment, and publication are separate future work.
